# Tandem: personal use terms

_Version 1. This is the same text the app shows on first launch (`crates/tandem-core/src/terms.rs`)._

Tandem is for personal, non-commercial DJing between friends. Only share music you have the right to use, keep what you receive private, and never use Tandem to make money.

1. **Personal, non-commercial use only.** Tandem is not for commercial use of any kind: no paid gigs, sponsored or monetised streams, client work, or business use.

2. **Only share music you have the right to use.** When you share a folder or crate, you confirm you own those files or are licensed to use them.

3. **Shared music stays private.** Tracks someone shares with you are for your own personal use. Don't sell, upload, re-share or redistribute them. Delete them if the person who shared them asks you to.

4. **Live sessions.** When your partner loads a track you don't have, Tandem copies it to your computer so you can hear the mix. These session copies are temporary and are deleted when Tandem closes.

5. **Broadcasting is your responsibility.** If you stream a set publicly, you are responsible for any permission or licence your platform or country requires.

6. **Copyright law still applies.** These terms are an agreement between the people using Tandem. They don't grant anyone rights in any music, and they don't make sharing legal where it otherwise isn't.

7. **No warranty.** Tandem is provided as is, without warranty. Its authors aren't liable for how it's used.

Tandem keeps a local record of what you agree to (consent-log.jsonl in its data folder). Nothing is sent anywhere except to the people in your room.
